# N95: WSOLA through the native ring

The current shell already contains SMLAD. A separate matched cohort,
[WSOLA68](N95_WSOLA_CURRENT_2026-10-04.md), measured it; below, 63 remains a
historical scalar comparison, and the samples are not mixed.

A separate candidate63 / host48 / protocol40 ran a pilot of 8 jobs and three
full series of 120 jobs each. All offsets, signed scores and the numbers of
checked positions match the scalar ARM reference for the eight audio cases,
including the extreme offsets and the tie case. In each job, 1280 words were
checked. [Results and journals](evidence/2026-10-04/n95-wsola63-results.json).

The ring has 16 KiB in total. A job passes four input slots (880 signed mono
words and zero padding) and receives one output slot. The DSP uses its own
880-word scratch in the node context. Native pages are kept between brackets;
ownership returns to ARM after completion and cache invalidate.
Delete/free/unmap/unreserve/close/FINAL0/EXIT0 succeeded in all runs. Six stock
clock checks before and after the series passed. These are snapshots; there is
no continuous DVFS check inside the batches.

The ARM baseline is the average of 16 warm scalar calls; it alternates before
and after the transfer. H+D is measured from the start of packing to reading
the result, and includes cache operations and notifications. Correctness is
checked outside this interval. Aggregate nearest-rank p50: **A=1155.853 us**,
**H+D=1983.643 us**. Only **16/360** paired samples have H+D<A before the launch
cost is amortized. This is a kernel test, not a measurement of emulator speedup.

Instrumented S = setup+teardown: p50 **828063 us**. Setup starts before
processor/DCD/task/buffer preparation; teardown covers the full cleanup. The
log synchronously performs Write+Flush for each line. Mapping and create, taken
separately, are much shorter, so the overall S cannot be called the pure cost of
create or the minimal cost of production integration. This test does not separate
the cause of all the delays. For the scenarios, the measured S of the
corresponding series was used.

The winning condition remains max(H+D,W)+S/N < A+W, that is, both strict
inequalities S/N<A and W>H+D+S/N−A. This host does not perform useful ARM W
while waiting for the DSP; the W scenarios are thresholds, not a measured
parallel speedup. Current conclusion: **keep the existing WSOLA**; under these
conditions there is no stable blocking gain. The SMLAD baseline is not measured;
the 135–200 us estimate is not mixed with the physical A. The MDEC/SPU ring
kernels are not yet measured.

720 DCR reads in the full series: 0000, WPE=0; this applies to candidate63. The
helper contains only MOV port(#0x200),T0 and RET, without configuration writes.
[Instruction audit](evidence/2026-10-04/n95-wsola63-instruction-audit.json).
The full independent OMAP2420 endpoint-publication contract is still open.
Raw DSP ticks and CLK_countspms=164 are kept without conversion to wall time.

**CPU usage is not qualified:** 320/360 GetCpuTime deltas exceed the
corresponding transfer wall interval. The cause is not established; the share
of offload CPU is not derived from these values. The next separate
[calibration without DSP](N95_CPU_COUNTER_2026-10-04.md) reproduced the delayed
slice accounting; a long aggregate window with settling boundaries is needed.
