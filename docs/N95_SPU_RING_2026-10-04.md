# N95 SPU67: exact plain-mix job through the ring

**For the measured blocking plain-mix job, keep it on the ARM.** The N95 ran
24 pilot jobs and 1080 full jobs in three series for 1/8/24 voices, 64
samples each voice. All 128 stereo accumulator words of each job matched the
independent Python oracle and the actual ARM assembler exactly; all pages,
ownership, headers, padding and lifecycle passed the checks. None of the 1080
full jobs had `H+D<A`, even without setup.

This is the **plain mix after ADSR**, not the whole SPU: ADPCM decode,
envelope/noise, pitch/fmod, reverb, capture, SPU RAM, DMA/register/IRQ
ordering and the final PCM remain outside the job. The emulator was not
changed; the E90 was not polled.

360 jobs of each size, 45 of each of the 8 fixtures. `H+D` starts before
packing and ends after the reconstruction of all 128 sums in ARM memory. It
includes input packing, cache operations, both notifications and the DSP
calculation. The oracle/header/padding/input-unchanged checks are outside
this boundary. The notification-only p50 = 1312.256/2502.441/2624.512 us is
not a clean DSP `D`; raw DSP ticks and counts_per_ms were not converted to ms.

`A` is the actual current HAVE_ARMV5 mixer with `smlawb/smlawt`, averaged
over 256 consecutive additions to warm sums. The reset, the single-job oracle
and the modular 256-job oracle are outside A; the voice wrapper/loop and the
volatile sink are inside. There is one ABI adaptation: the global ChanBuf
load was replaced with a fifth pointer argument; the arithmetic instructions
were kept. The live emulator cache/state differs from the isolated probe.
ARM-before/after alternates across jobs.

A min/max for 1/8/24 voices: 3.576–16.332/13.471–44.346/
40.412–98.109 us. The spread is retained in the pooled/per-case evidence. The
eight stock clock brackets are snapshots, not a continuous DVFS trace; these
measurements do not establish the cause of the change in times within a
series. The profile was not changed.

## Data contract and arithmetic

Ring 16 KiB TOTAL: 1024 B control, 15 input and 15 output slots of 512 B,
480 B payload per slot. The input contains 128 initial 32-bit sums, 64
samples32 of each voice, and the left/right volume32. The output is 128
sums32. The 24-voice input of 6848 B nearly exhausts the 7200 B input payload.
Pointers are not passed through the DSP.

The Python oracle reproduces the packed OR/shift of the volume, the signed
high-product SMLAW and the modular 32-bit accumulation, including the spill
from a negative left volume. The DSP uses a native MPY/MAC decomposition
32×16; the 40-bit C55 long long does not replace the required signed 48-bit
product. The ROM `__mpyli` is not imported; all 15 imports are in the saved
N95 baseimage. The fixtures cover zero, signed 16-bit edges,
sparse/random samples, negative/fractional/zero gains, and initial sum
wrap-around at 32 bits. Two fixtures additionally check full signed 32-bit
samples and volumes outside the legal PCM range. This is synthetic
correctness coverage, not captured gameplay, and not an exhaustive proof for
all possible inputs.

In the full series, 138240 stereo sums32 and 2672640 ring words16 were
checked; the 24 pilot jobs are counted separately. Before and after each job,
the DCR port 0200 was only read: 2160 readings, 0000/WPE 0. The audit checks
the collected helper bytes `A45102004804` without relocations/writes. DCR
reads may affect ordering; the independent OMAP2420 external-memory
publication contract remains open. The successful fixtures do not close it.

## Setup and asynchronous scenarios

Instrumented `S=setup+teardown`: p50 = 863494 us, min 817107 / max 1203368 us,
9 full runs. It includes lifecycle journaling, not the minimum production
startup. Useful independent ARM `W=0`; CPU usage was not measured (`-1`).

Strict formula: `max(H+D,W)+S/N < A+W`, that is, simultaneously `S/N<A` and
`W>H+D+S/N−A`. For N16/64/256/1000, the first condition did not hold in any
job. Even the hypothetical N1000000, under which it held in all 1080, gives
no blocking gain; the p50 required W for 1/8/24 voices is about
1614.750/3269.419/3500.830 us. These are only arithmetic thresholds, not
measured independent work, and not proof of the payback of a resident or
asynchronous SPU.

The full SPU RAM 512 KiB and the state coherency require a different job
boundary: [ABI/capacity audit](N95_SPU_BOUNDARY_2026-10-04.md). The historical
scene 3.7% budget does not replace the actual A of this probe. Full PCM/state
equivalence remains unmeasured; there is no basis for moving the whole SPU now.
