# N95: CPU cost of the whole ring window

After the [counter calibration](N95_CPU_COUNTER_2026-10-04.md), the new
candidate64/host49/protocol38 completed a pilot of 120 packets and three full
series, batch 1/2/8/15: 12 runs, 9360 packets. All words, wrap/carry, native
pages, ownership and teardown are correct; six stock clock snapshots PASS.
[Results/journals](evidence/2026-10-04/n95-ring-cpu64-results.json).

The CPU baseline was taken after settling with User::After(1); the final yield
and both GetCpuTime calls are included in the outer wall. Inside the 120-job
window there is no Log Write/Flush, no ARM reference baseline and no per-frame
CPU reads. The journal is written from a retained 120-row array after the
measurement. CPU includes pack/cache, notification calls, waits, the full
input/output check, bookkeeping and the final yield. Therefore this is the cost
of the correctness harness, not pure wait CPU or a production workload. The
final yield is explicitly measured and is not subtracted from the denominator
of the CPU fraction. In all 13 windows, CPU fits within the outer wall with a
tolerance of two ticks (~61 us) for quantization.

Useful bidirectional bytes = 480 payload bytes ×2×packets. The exchange interval
starts after pack and ends after completion/cache invalidate; the full window
also includes pack/full-word checks/final yield. These two throughput
definitions are not interchangeable. Useful parallel ARM W=0. The per-frame CPU
field=-1 means no measurement, not negative CPU usage.

DSP64 was rebuilt from the current source; its SHA differs from the historical
52/61; the typed files and archive of the old cohorts were not replaced.
Therefore the new performance samples are separate. In 64, DCR is not read, and
WPE is not attributed to this cohort from pilot 62/WSOLA63. DSP raw ticks were
not converted to ms. The frequency brackets are snapshots; there is no
continuous DVFS trace or performance reservation. This synthetic transform does
not prove an application kernel speedup.

The N95 CPU criterion now has a measurement with calibrated boundaries and an
explicitly limited scope; the old short-delta percentages remain unqualified.
The independent OMAP2420 endpoint-publication contract is still open, and D1/D2
overall are in_progress. The MDEC/SPU actual ring kernels and the application W
are not measured; the negative blocking verdict of WSOLA63 remains separate.
